FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything operators ask us about implementation, pricing, and our Salem headquarters.

How long does implementation actually take?
Most customers reach full live production in four to eight weeks, depending on the number of facilities, existing TMS/WMS software, and custom carrier integrations involved.
Do we have to replace our existing TMS or WMS?
No. Stackly acts as an overlay management system that connects with SAP, Oracle, Manhattan Associates, and custom ERP systems via REST APIs & Webhooks without disrupting your legacy workflow.
Where is Stackly's primary technology & operations hub located?
Our primary operations and logistics technology hub is based in Salem, Tamil Nadu, India. We also operate key regional hubs in Chennai, Bengaluru, and Coimbatore.
What is the pricing structure for Stackly logistics management?
We offer flexible subscription plans tailored to monthly shipment volume, managed warehouse square footage, or enterprise SLA requirements. Contact our Salem sales team for a custom quote.
How fast is your customer support response time?
Our 24/7 priority emergency hotline answers calls in under 30 seconds. Online inquiry forms and support tickets are answered within 15 minutes by our Salem dispatch engineering team.
Does Stackly provide real-time GPS tracking and sensor telematics?
Yes. All shipments feature live GPS tracking, temperature telematics for cold chain storage, and automated geofence notification alerts sent via SMS, Email, or WhatsApp.
Can we schedule a physical visit to the Salem Logistics Hub?
Yes! We welcome enterprise clients and logistics partners to tour our Salem facility. You can schedule a visit by calling +91 94426 73815 or emailing hello@stackly.com.
Is our operational and shipment data secure?
Stackly uses enterprise-grade SOC-2 Type II compliant cloud infrastructure with end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
