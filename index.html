FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things operators ask us most often, in the order they ask them.

How long does implementation actually take?
Most customers reach production in four to eight weeks, depending on the number of facilities and carriers involved. We work in staged waves — nothing cuts over until the previous wave has run clean for two full cycles.
Do we have to replace our existing TMS or WMS?
No. Convoy sits between your existing systems and your carriers, providing the operational model and visibility layer on top. Your TMS keeps doing what it does; Convoy makes sure the plan and the execution actually match.
What happens if a carrier fails or a route breaks?
Every route carries a declared fallback. When a carrier misses a pickup or a lane becomes unavailable, the platform routes to the secondary and records the deviation in the audit log. Nothing silently disappears.
Can we export our data if we leave?
Yes, always. Every record on the platform is exportable as structured data — shipments, routes, contracts, audit entries, everything. There is no lock-in and no exit fee.
Do you offer multi-region support?
Yes. The platform provisions across regions from the same topology spec, so a deployment in AP-South and one in EU-West share the same model, the same policies, and the same audit format.
What is the minimum contract length?
Monthly for Starter and Professional. Enterprise engagements are typically annual with a stated review window. We do not lock customers into multi-year contracts they cannot exit.
